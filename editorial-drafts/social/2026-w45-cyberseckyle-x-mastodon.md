## CK-SOC-20261106-03 | Fri Nov 6 | Release notes
Status: Needs review. Evidence: Original product communication advice. CTA: Discussion, no URL. Asset: None.

### X
Release notes that say 'bug fixes and improvements' miss the point. Tell people what changed, who might notice, and whether they need to do anything. Good release notes reduce uncertainty after an update. #BuildInPublic

### Mastodon
I get why small apps end up with release notes that only say 'bug fixes and improvements.' Writing the useful version takes more thought.

But when someone updates, they want to know what changed, whether it affects their workflow, and if they need to do anything. A few specific sentences beat a long list of internal tickets.

#BuildInPublic #IndieDev
