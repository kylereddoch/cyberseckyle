Weekly Notes: Short week, full recharge (Week 52, 2025)

Weekly Notes, Week 52: holiday downtime, light work alerts, ear healing update, movie bingeing, and a new LEGO Gameboy on the shelf.

Published on December 29, 2025 at 9:00am by Kyle Reddoch • 1 min read

TThis week’s note is shorter than usual since it was a holiday week and everything ran on “short week” mode.

The vibe

Last week was steady in the best way. I only worked through Wednesday, and work stayed mostly quiet outside of the usual alerts and the occasional support call. Personally, I did what I’ve needed for a while: relaxed, watched a bunch of movies, and took it easy. No regrets.

Highlights

  • Cleared out a bunch of apps I haven’t used in forever. Tiny declutter, instant satisfaction.
  • Ear update: I’d say I’m about 90% healed. Still get the “underwater” feeling now and then, but overall it’s way better.
  • It was nice having zero schoolwork hanging over my head. I’m ready to be done with school for good.
  • The extra days off did exactly what they were supposed to: recharge.

Life

Even with the extra time off, the Linux box that’s going to become my new Plex server only made it as far as my office. I didn’t do much tech tinkering over the break. Mostly I just vegged out and enjoyed the slower pace.

  • Finally built the LEGO Game Boy set. Super fun build and it looks awesome on my office shelf. I didn’t grab a photo yet, but I’ll post one on Mastodon soon.
  • I watched a lot of movies over the break. Some were great. Some… not so much.

Security corner

Because it was a short week, I didn’t go deep on any new security topics. It was mostly routine monitoring and alert triage. I’ll be back in that mode after the new year.

Worth sharing

  • I’m working on a cybersecurity thriller. Yep, for real. It’s still early, but it’s been a fun side project to tinker with. I’ll share updates on Mastodon and in these weekly notes as it takes shape.

That is it for this week. I hope you have a great week and remember to be kind to each other!
