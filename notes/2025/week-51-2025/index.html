Weekly Notes: Less chaotic, more grounded (Week 51, 2025)

Weekly Notes, Week 51: life stuff, tech tinkering, SaaS Alerts at work, and the security topics on my radar—kept simple, personal, and grounded.

Published on December 23, 2025 by Kyle Reddoch • 2 min read

I am trying out a new format for these posts. Let me know what you think!

Yesterday was a busy day, I got a call before I was even to work. The day didn’t stop after that. I wasn’t able to write this post yesterday so here is to posting it with a delay. Hopefully, it doesn’t become a pattern but no promises.

The vibe

Last week slowed down a bit, which was nice. It gave me some time to relax and recharge. Overall vibe was less chaotic and more grounded.

Highlights

  • I found out that Stargate SG1 is leaving Prime Video on December 31st. I just started watching it. I am not sure if another streaming service will pick it up, but I’m hoping it will be available on another platform soon.
  • The ruptured ear drum started to heal. There were days that I couldn’t hear and then some that the feeling was back and I could hear a bit more. I am so ready for this to be healed and back to normal.
  • My oldest daughter’s phone finally pooped the bed. I guess it couldn’t take her dropping it anymore. I helped her with getting a new iPhone 17 Pro.
  • Energy dipped midweek, so I leaned on checklists instead of willpower.

Build

  • Shipped: We added SaaS Alerts to our platform at work. We now can notify our users when something is wrong or iffy with their SaaS integrtations. I am still getting all of this set up and will probably be after the new year.
  • Learned: SaaS monitoring only helps if the alert routing is sane, noise is the enemy.
  • Stuck: Still getting side tracked on some of the tasks that I need to get done. I am trying to find a way to stay focused and get them done.

Life

  • 🍿 I started working on moving my Plex server to a linux box. I am using an old Dell Optiplex 780 that we had lying around at work and couldn’t be updated to Windows 11. I’ve been using Plex on a Windows box for years, but I’ve wanted to switch it to linux for awhile now.
  • 🏎️ I can’t wait for January 1st. Lego is releasing the APXGP Speed Champion Car from F1 the Movie. This will be a great one to add to my collection.
  • 📱 I started playing a new mobile game called Foundation: Galactic Frontier. I have gotten quite addicted to it.

Security corner

  • Hardened: did a quick SaaS hygiene pass: removed stale access, verified the “who gets alerted” path, and cut noise so real issues stand out.
  • Watched: browser-extension risk and supply-chain incidents, especially how quickly “helpful” add-ons turn into a foothold.
  • Thinking about: how to make “secure by default” feel effortless for normal users, not like homework. Maybe even a post will come of it.

Worth sharing

  • 🎶 Found this neat tool that builds a Spotify Wrapped style graphic using your scrobbler data from ListenBrainz, Last.fm, or even Navidrome.
  • How to Stay Safe on Your New AI Browser — If you use AI browser like Comet or Atlas, understanding how to keep yourself safe is very important.
  • Make sure to check out the Security Now Podcast for some great content.

That is it for this week. I hope you have a great week and remember to be kind to each other!
