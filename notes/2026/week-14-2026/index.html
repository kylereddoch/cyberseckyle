A New Look, a Quiet Weekend, and a Full Heart (Week 14, 2026)

Week 14 was a mix of creative momentum, meaningful reflection, and much-needed rest, from launching my new blog design to slowing down for a spiritually important week.

Published on April 7, 2026 by Kyle Reddoch • 2 min read

Coming in a day late with this one again but it is for a good reason. I was busy launching my new design yesterday.

The vibe

This week felt grounded in the best way. There was a lot of energy poured into launching my new blog design, but it was balanced out by a quieter weekend, some real physical reset, and time to reflect during a spiritually important week. Overall, it felt like one of those weeks where progress and pause came together nicely.

Highlights

  • I went to my chiropractor for my regular adjustment visit. Man do these feel good. This time I had them pull (decompression) on my lumbar area (the past two times I have had them pull on my neck). They started with a light pull so I didn’t really feel it. After leaving, I did start to notice my numbness in my left leg after work and into the evening that I havent had that much in a while. I am not sure if the pull caused a nerve to relocate or what.
  • I worked a my new design for the blog pretty much all week. I wanted to get a design that was more IndieWeb friendly and more focused on the content and didn’t feel like some generic template. I am really happy with the way it turned out.
  • This week was a special week. It was the Memorial Week of the death of Jesus. As a Christian, this is a very important week for me. It is a time to reflect on the sacrifice that Jesus made for us and to remember the love that he has for us. The memorial landing on Thursday this year and it was a really good service. It was nice to be reminded of the benefits that Jesus’ death has for us.
  • I did absolutely nothing this weekend. I stayed home and just relaxed. I just felt like slumming it. 😁
  • I have been watching The Man in the High Castle. It is a show about an alternate history where the Axis powers won World War II. It is a really good show and I have been enjoying it a lot. It is a great show and I would definitely recommend it if you are looking for something to watch.

Worth sharing

  • Came across this post on Mastodon for this open source app that allows you to safely test PDFs. I will be checking this out for sure.
  • HaveIBeenPwned got a new feature for passkeys.
  • I wrote my thoughts on the recent 2026 Cyber Protect Report from SonicWall.

✌️ That is it for this week. I hope you have a great week and remember to be kind to each other!
