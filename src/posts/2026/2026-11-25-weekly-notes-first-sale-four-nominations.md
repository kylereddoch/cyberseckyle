## The vibe

Feeling pretty great and accomplished. It has been a busy week, but the good kind of busy. I have had plenty to keep me occupied, and it feels good to see things coming together.

## Highlights

* &#x1F389; **I finally got my first direct sale of [Trayage](https://trayage.app/)!** Somebody picked it up using the Black Friday offer. I know it is one sale, but I have been trying to get to this point for a while. I am still very much figuring out the marketing side of all this. LOL.

* &#x1F3C6; **Trayage is nominated in four categories at the MacPip Awards:** Best Utility, Best Free App, Best Pay-Once App, and Hidden Gem. I shared [the voting details on the Trayage site](https://trayage.app/blog/trayage-macpip-awards-2026/). If you have been using it and want to give it a vote, I would appreciate it!

* &#x1F6E0;&#xFE0F; **I put out a small Trayage update.** The review window had a problem with dragging it around, which is one of those basic things that needs to just work. The [direct update went out](https://trayage.app/changelog/#version-1-1-3-build-14), and I sent the App Store version to Apple too.

* &#x1F4BB; **I have also been tinkering with a few other app ideas.** I am keeping the details to myself for now, but there is plenty to keep me busy on that front.

* &#x1F50E; **There was client work in the mix, too.** I spent time digging through endpoint alerts and working on a disk diagnostic script. That also meant sorting through the output and turning it into something readable. A wall of diagnostic text is not much fun for the next person who has to look at it.

## Worth sharing

* **[NuKey](https://nukeyapp.com/)** has been a fun one. I have been using it for a couple of weeks now and really like it. I have even made a few custom keyboards that other people are using. If you like tinkering with how your phone looks and feels, give it a look.
* **[Showcase](https://getshowcase.app/)** has become my go-to for keeping track of the shows and movies I watch. It is really nicely made, and it works well on the Duo too. I gave it [another shout-out on X](https://x.com/thecyberseckyle/status/2125357955772264597) this week because it deserves one.
* I wrote **[An Outlook Spoofing Advisory Isn't a Complete Incident Story](/blog/outlook-spoofing-advisory-msp-verification/)** about the questions behind “I opened it in Outlook” and what still needs checking after an update is installed.
* On the Trayage site, **[ZIP Files in Mac Downloads: Decide What to Keep Before Cleanup](https://trayage.app/blog/review-zip-archives-mac-downloads/)** looks at the ZIP files and extracted folders that tend to pile up in Downloads. Having both does not automatically mean one is ready to go.

&#x270C;&#xFE0F; That is it for this week. I hope you have a great week and remember to be kind to each other!
