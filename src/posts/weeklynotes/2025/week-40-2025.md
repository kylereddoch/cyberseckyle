## Tennis

My daughter signed up to play tennis this year in school. This is the first time she has play tennis, and any sport for that matter. Morning practics start at 7:00am, which is early for her. The coaches want them there at 6:50am to make sure they are ready to go at 7:00am. I have been driving her to practice, and it has been fun to see her learning a new sport. I stay and watch her practice everyday, and I can see the improvement in her skills. She still has a long way to go, but she is enjoying the process.

I, of course, am becoming that dad who is getting all into the sport. I have been watching a lot of tennis matches on TV and reading about the rules and strategies of the game. I even bought a tennis racket and some balls to practice with her. I am looking forward to seeing her play in her first match in a couple of weeks.

## Brewventy, an Eleventy starter kit

I released my first open source project last week. It is called [Brewventy](https://github.com/cyberseckyle/brewventy), and it is a coffee-themed starter kit for Eleventy. I am really excited about this project, and I hope it helps other people get started with Eleventy. It is simple, flexible, and powerful. I wanted to create a starter kit that would make it easy for people to get started with Eleventy, and I think Brewventy does that.
