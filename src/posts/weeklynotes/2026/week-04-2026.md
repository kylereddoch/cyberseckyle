## The vibe

A fake-apocalypse snow week that still managed to bite, held together with space heaters, nostalgia dopamine, and the sheer satisfaction of typing “draft complete.”

## Highlights

* I survived **SNOWMAGEDDON 2026**! Most of the weather forecasts predicted upwards of 10" of snow for my area. People went crazy stocking up on groceries. You know the usual drill. We ended up getting not even 2" of snow. Total bust!
* This snowstorm did bring hella cold weather, though. Like "feel like" temps in the negatives. Brrr.
* Of course our kitchen pipes frozen, they always do this when it gets this cold. Even had them dripping! I hate this house we rent. It had NO insulation in the walls. We had to run space heaters all night to thaw them out.
* I ordered a [new phone case](/blog/bondi-blue-in-my-pocket/) for my 17 Pro Max. **_Total nostalgia trip_**. I am loving it so much, it's probably on of the best cases I have had.
* I finished the draft of my book, **The FOIS Files: The CIPHER Fellows**. I am so relieved to have it done. Now I just need to edit it and get it ready for publishing. I am looking into self-publishing options.
* Added some changes to my website. **Can you spot them?** 😉 I also updated the /now section to be included in the blog listings and RSS feed.

## Work

* Lately I've been having a hard time staying focused at work. It's like my brain is in a fog. I just sit there staring at my screen, unable to concentrate. It's frustrating.
* I have been trying to work on getting our SaaS Alerts system setup at work. Trying to get the IOCs, rules, and filters configured.
* Last week was fun with the Microsoft O365 outage. We had a few clients reach out about email issues, _but luckily it was a global problem and not something on our end_.
