## The vibe

Excited, overheated, and running on fumes.

I am genuinely excited about what I spent the week building. I just wish I had felt better while doing it.

## Highlights

* &#x1F4F0; The biggest part of my week went into creating **The Defender’s Dispatch**, a new CybersecKyle newsletter. The first issue lands this Friday, August 7.
* &#x1F6E0;&#xFE0F; Starting a newsletter involved a lot more than writing the first issue. I worked through the name, design, publishing workflow, signup process, and the rest of the pieces needed to make it something I can send every week. You can [read more about what will be in The Defender’s Dispatch](/blog/introducing-the-defenders-dispatch/) or [sign up for your weekly copy](/newsletter/).
* &#x1F3A5; Saturday was a daddy-daughter day with my 13-year-old. We went to see the new *Spider-Man: Brand New Day* movie and then grabbed brunch. It was a good break from staring at newsletter workflows and security news.
* &#x1F321;&#xFE0F; The heat is still miserable. We have had horribly high temperatures for what feels like forever, and I am more than ready for someone to send some snow our way.
* &#x1F912; Between the heat and everything else, I have been worn out and feeling sick most days. I got quite a bit done, but I definitely did not feel great doing it.

## Worth sharing

* I started the Blue Team Fundamentals track of my how-to series with a practical [Windows workstation baseline using Defender and ASR rules](/blog/cyberseckyle-security-how-to-series-blue-team-fundamentals-part-1-windows-workstation-baseline-with-defender-and-asr-rules/), then followed it with a [macOS baseline covering profiles and audit basics](/blog/cyberseckyle-security-how-to-series-blue-team-fundamentals-part-2-macos-baseline-with-profiles-and-audit-basics/).
* The latest [Security Signal Weekly](/blog/security-signal-weekly-july-25-31-2026/) covered exploited firewall-management flaws, attacks against water systems, AI-agent risks, and the other stories defenders should have on their radar.
* I boosted BleepingComputer’s report on [Cisco’s actively exploited Secure Firewall Management Center flaw](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/cisco-warns-of-fmc-static-credential-flaw-exploited-in-zero-day-attacks/). Cisco released hotfixes, and there is no workaround, so this is one to check if you manage FMC.
* On the lighter side, I also boosted [a browser user-agent prank aimed at AI crawlers](https://infosec.exchange/@cR0w/117005396523521830). Changing the user agent to ClaudeBot so every blocked request looks like more AI nonsense is the right amount of internet shenanigans.

&#x270C;&#xFE0F; That is it for this week. I hope you have a great week and remember to be kind to each other!
