I missed last week's notes, so I am combining Weeks 45 and 46 into one post. These cover November 2 through November 15.

## The vibe

A mix of fun and frustration. I am still having a great time with the iPhone Duo and working on new ideas, but my health has had its ups and downs. That is always a battle, and I am trying to stay strong through it.

## Highlights

* &#x1F4F1; **I am still having hella fun with the iPhone Duo.** This phone is amazing. The more I use it, the less sure I am that I could ever go back to a slab phone again.

* &#x1F4AC; **I decided to go back to X/Twitter.** I have been posting more there lately and getting way more engagement there and on Mastodon.

* &#x1F50E; **Work has included tracking down phishing attempts at client sites.** A few involved PDFs in emails, so I have been digging into those. I have also been setting up Conditional Access rules for a client after our SaaS Alerts monitoring picked up sign-in alerts from different countries. Those alerts were a reason to take a closer look at the client's sign-in controls.

* &#x1F3E0; **I am still on the work-from-home wagon.** Not complaining, though.

* &#x1F4BB; **The solo founder work continues.** I am still trying to get the first sale of my Mac app. I am by no means a marketer. LOL. I have also been working on a few app and SaaS ideas, so there is plenty to keep me busy on that front.

### Week 45

* &#x270D;&#xFE0F; **There was a lot of writing.** I published reviews of [TypeDraw Notebook](/blog/typedraw-notebook-review/), [Showcase](/blog/showcase-movie-tv-tracker-review/), [MoneyCoach](/blog/moneycoach-budget-planner-review/), [Lume](/blog/lume-recipe-manager-review/), and [Cassette Player](/blog/cassette-player-review/). I also wrote about an [LXD MicroCloud certificate bypass](/blog/lxd-microcloud-certificate-bypass-msp/) and put together a [Conditional Access rollout checklist for MSPs](/blog/conditional-access-rollout-msp-checklist/).

* &#x1F4F0; **Security Signal Weekly was back on Friday.** The [October 31–November 6 edition](/blog/security-signal-weekly-october-31-november-6-2026/) rounded up the week's security news for defenders and MSPs.

### Week 46

* &#x1F4EC; **I ended The Defender’s Dispatch after 13 issues.** Putting together both the web edition and the email every week had become stressful, and I did not want to keep promising a schedule that was wearing on me. I am glad I made it, and I appreciate everyone who read, subscribed, or sent a story. [Issue 013](/newsletter/defenders-dispatch/issue-013/2026-11-09/) was the last one; the [archive](/newsletter/archive/) will stay online. I wrote a [short note about the decision](/notes/ending-the-defenders-dispatch/), too. I will keep writing here, including Security Signal Weekly.

* &#x1F512; **I wrote about what comes after a security alert or a patch.** The [SonicWall SSL VPN article](/blog/sonicwall-sslvpn-saml-bypass-msp-verification/) looks at the authentication paths MSPs need to check after patching the SAML bypass. Later in the week, I wrote a [quarantine investigation checklist](/blog/endpoint-quarantine-investigation-checklist-msp/) for working through what a security product actually contained, how a file arrived, and what still needs checking before a ticket closes.

* &#x2601;&#xFE0F; **The Microsoft 365 outage became another article.** I looked at the [November 12–13 outage and its impact on businesses and schools](/blog/microsoft-365-global-outage-business-schools-msps/), including what IT teams and MSPs can do when the service they rely on for customer communication is having trouble itself.

* &#x2B50; **I added a Tech Review badge to the site.** Articles tagged as reviews now show the badge in listings and on the article itself, making those posts easier to spot.

&#x270C;&#xFE0F; That is it for these past two weeks. I hope you have a great week and remember to be kind to each other!
