> This one is a few days late. Most of my free time last week went into personal projects, and I kept choosing "one more thing" on the server over sitting down to write this. LOL.

## The vibe

Restless, but in a good way.

My brain has been stuck in that mode where it keeps finding one more thing to build, fix, or improve. It has made for some late nights, but it also feels good to be excited about personal projects again.

## Highlights

* &#x1F4FA; I finally have my Jellyfin server up and running. It is on Ubuntu using an old Dell Precision T1700 that I salvaged, and I have spent quite a bit of time setting up the plugins and customizations. So far, it is running great.
* &#x1F310; I installed Caddy so I can provide remote access through my AT&T connection. The plan is to let my parents and brother stream from the server too, which will make all of this even more useful.
* &#x1F512; I also installed Tailscale. I am not doing much with it yet, but I plan to use it when I move my DNS setup over later.
* &#x1F6E0;&#xFE0F; A few scripts and apps came out of the media-server project. They started as tools to help me rip DVDs, transfer files to my NAS, and manage the library, but I figured other people might find them useful too. I released [ReelArrange](/projects/reelarrange/), [RipFoundry for Linux](/projects/ripfoundry-for-linux/), and [RipFoundry for Windows](/projects/ripfoundry-for-windows/).
* &#x1F4BF; I am still working through our DVDs and my parents' collection so I can move everything into Jellyfin. It is going to take a while, but I am looking forward to having all of it available digitally.
* &#x1F3CA; We went to the city pool Saturday. Floating around the lazy river and cooling off was a really nice break from the heat and all the projects.
* &#x1F3E0; I have been working from home for the past three weeks. The office has been around 84 degrees, and I finally told my boss I could not keep working in that heat. I will not be going back until something is done about it.
* &#x1F4F0; The first issue of **The Defender's Dispatch** went out to subscribers Friday! If you are not subscribed yet, [what are you waiting for?](/newsletter/) LOL.
