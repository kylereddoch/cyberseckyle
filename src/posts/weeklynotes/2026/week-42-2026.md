## The vibe

A little behind on writing this one, but last week deserves a catch-up.

There was plenty of app work, but the highlight was getting to Lubbock, spending time with my daughter, and seeing an absolutely amazing concert. I also learned that standing through the entire thing was a decision my back would remember for several days. Apparently enthusiasm does not count as a pain management plan. LOL.

## Highlights

* &#x1F3B6; We went to Lubbock for **Texas Tech's homecoming bonfire**, which also included a concert with **Bowling for Soup and the Goo Goo Dolls**. It was absolutely amazing. Getting to spend some time with my daughter made the trip even better. I [shared a few photos on X](https://x.com/thecyberseckyle/status/2111460226608279789) if you want a look at the night.
* &#x1F363; We also went to an **all-you-can-eat sushi bar**, and I loved it. Sushi, noodles, teriyaki chicken and steak... I ate way too much. There were apparently quite a few things I needed to try before I could admit I was full.
* &#x1F634; The next few days were rough. I stood for the entire concert, pushed myself too much, and my back paid the price. I was worn out and hurting afterward. I am glad we went, but next time I need to put a little more thought into the standing-for-hours part.
* &#x1F680; **[Trayage 1.1](https://trayage.app/blog/trayage-1-1/) went out**, following the Mac App Store arrival I mentioned in the previous notes. This update brought a redesigned overview, smart filters, local duplicate checks, and new ways to check Downloads from the menu bar. I also lowered the one-time price from US$29.99 to **US$9.99**. Review stays free, and cleanup still requires choosing files and confirming before anything moves to Trash.
* &#x1F3A8; I spent a lot of time on **Trayage's app and website design**. There is a new violet palette and tray icon, and I reworked the website to give the actual app screenshots more room. Feedback has been useful here: someone arriving for the first time needs to see what Trayage does and understand what they are being asked to try. Getting that explanation right takes work too.
* &#x1F4F1; **iPhone Duo preorder day was also a whole thing.** The AT&T experience did not go well, but a heads-up on X helped me find release-day pickup through Best Buy. I cancelled the AT&T order and [got that sorted](https://x.com/thecyberseckyle/status/2111117736181186852). A little more excitement than I needed before the trip!
