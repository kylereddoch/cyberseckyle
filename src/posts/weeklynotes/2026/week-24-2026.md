## The vibe

This was a very Apple-heavy week. WWDC26 happened, I watched the keynote, I put developer betas on devices I actually use because apparently I enjoy chaos, and then Siri AI sent my security brain down several rabbit holes. Add in summer heat showing up way too early and a great F1 weekend, and it was a full week.

## Highlights

* &#x1F34E; Last week was WWDC26, so of course I spent a couple of hours at work watching the keynote and checking out the new OS updates coming later this year. I always enjoy seeing what Apple is doing, even when it immediately gives me a dozen security questions.
* &#x1F4F1; I downloaded the new developer betas on my iPhone and MacBook. I always live on the wild side. So far, it has been fun poking around, waiting on the new Siri AI access, and finding the random beta weirdness that comes with early builds.
* &#x1F9E0; The Siri AI announcements were interesting enough that I ended up writing a whole series of articles about how Apple's new AI direction could affect cybersecurity, MSPs, personal context, prompt injection, app actions, scams, and business policy. It is exciting tech, but it definitely changes the risk model.
* &#x1F4BC; Work-wise, the thing I kept coming back to was how AI exposes the gaps between tools, people, and process. I wrote about that from the MSP angle in [AI Is Exposing the Operational Debt Inside MSP Security Stacks](/blog/ai-is-exposing-the-operational-debt-inside-msp-security-stacks/). AI can move fast, but if the underlying workflow is messy, it mostly helps you make messy decisions faster.
* &#x1F9F0; I also launched [EmojiCodec](/blog/introducing-emojicodec-a-bidirectional-emoji-converter-for-vs-code/), a small VS Code extension I built to convert emoji between native characters, HTML entities, Unicode escapes, hexadecimal values, and Markdown shortcodes. It started as something I wanted for my own workflow and turned into something I decided to share.
* &#x1F525; It has been crazy hot here, and summer has barely started. I am not looking forward to this weather. I am already in the "stay inside and avoid becoming soup" part of the year.
* &#x1F3CE;&#xFE0F; The Barcelona-Catalunya Grand Prix was last weekend. It is always a great weekend when there is F1, and this one was extra special with Lewis getting his first win in Ferrari. Great race and a very cool moment to watch.
