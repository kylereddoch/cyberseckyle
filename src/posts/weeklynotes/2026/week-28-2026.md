## The vibe

Low battery week.

Not bad. Not memorable. Just warm, slow, and a little foggy. I was not really feeling like myself, and most of the week had that "I am here, but not fully here" feeling.

## Highlights

* &#x1F321;&#xFE0F; The office was way too hot, and I was already feeling run down, so I took a couple of days off toward the end of the week. I do not always do a great job stepping away before I am completely drained, but this was one of those times where I needed to.
* &#x1F4BB; I had one of those work-computer moments where you are staring at the screen and wondering why you are still there. That probably sums up the middle of the week better than anything else.
* &#x1F6E1;&#xFE0F; I also had a SonicWall acting strange enough that it looked like it was blinking SOS in Morse code. I have worked around plenty of firewalls, but that was a new one for me.
* &#x1F4DD; Even with the week feeling slow, I did get some writing out the door. Sometimes that is the only productive thing that feels realistic, and I will take it.
