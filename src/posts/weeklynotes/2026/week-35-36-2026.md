> The past two weeks got away from me. We found a new house, moved almost immediately, and have been trying to get settled ever since, so I decided to combine Weeks 35 and 36 into one post.

## The vibe

Whiplashed, exhausted, and relieved.

Everything happened much faster than I expected, and moving is never fun, but we are in a better place and a better neighborhood now. There is still plenty to unpack and put back together, but things are finally starting to feel a little more normal.

## Highlights

### Week 35

* &#x1F3E0; We found a new house! We were just starting to look and found one we liked, but I did not expect them to call us back the same day and ask us to come sign the lease. It all moved really fast.
* &#x1F4E6; We ended up having to move that same weekend. I will spare y'all the boring details, but it was a very fast move. I always hate moving. It is never fun!
* &#x1F3D8;&#xFE0F; The good news is that we are in a better place and a better neighborhood, so there is definitely that.

### Week 36

* &#x1F4F6; Of course, the internet transition was a total nightmare. It took three days to get the internet back up at the new place. Come on, AT&T.
* &#x1F6CB;&#xFE0F; We are still getting everything situated and settled in. It is starting to feel like home, but there are still plenty of boxes and loose ends to work through.
* &#x1F5A5;&#xFE0F; I still need to put my office back together and get my homelab set back up. I am ready to have my normal workspace again.
* &#x270D;&#xFE0F; With the move happening so quickly, I have not gotten much writing out the door. That has been frustrating, but I also know I needed to give myself time to get through the move and settle in.
