## Work

* It was the beginning of a new month, so we had our monthly AV deep scan that ran. Therefore, I spent time reviewing the results/tickets that came in from that. Those are always fun to review because you get to see all the weird things that are happening on endpoints across the fleet. It's like a little window into the chaos of the digital world.
* I also worked on a [maintenance task on our RMM](/blog/dont-do-this-how-i-accidentally-simulated-attacker-clea) that runs a series of scripts to clear various things on our managed endpoints; like caches, temp files, check system time, etc. I scheduled it to run at 1am on Sundays, but I forgot that I also included a script to clean Windows Log files. This would trigger the "logs cleared" alert in our SOC. So, I accidentally simulated an attacker cleanup across our fleet. Oops! It was a good reminder of how important logs are for security and incident response.
