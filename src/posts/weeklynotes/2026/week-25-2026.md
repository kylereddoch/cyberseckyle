## The vibe

This was one of those weeks that had some fun tech stuff, some meaningful spiritual time, and one really hard life update right in the middle of it all. I am trying to focus on the good things where I can, but honestly, the job and insurance scramble has been a lot.

## Highlights

* &#x231A; The new Apple Watch Ultra 3 I ordered came in. I got it set up and, of course, installed the watchOS 27 beta on it. Because apparently I cannot just leave a brand-new device alone for five minutes.
* &#x1F614; Unfortunately, my wife lost her job last week. Her job of 16 years. She has dedicated most of her working career to that place, and for it to end this way really stings. We are now scrambling through all the practical stuff too, especially since she was the one carrying our insurance.
* &#x1F64F; We had our [Regional Convention](https://www.jw.org/en/jehovahs-witnesses/conventions/) this past weekend. It was good to be there, but I was pretty tired afterward. The health issues with my back and neck were definitely reminding me that long weekends are still long weekends.
* &#x1F4BB; I started working on my first macOS app. I am not ready to share details yet, but it has been fun getting deeper into SwiftUI and macOS development. I am still deciding whether I want to release it on the App Store or independently. If you would like to beta test it when it is ready, just [hit me up](/contact/).
* &#x1F4DD; Writing-wise, I finished out a pretty long run of Apple Intelligence and Siri AI security posts. That whole topic keeps branching into privacy, MSP policy, prompt injection, app actions, cloud trust, image generation, cameras, and business device management.

## Worth sharing

* I wrote about why businesses and MSPs need to think through Apple Intelligence before it quietly becomes normal on work devices in [Before Siri AI Hits Work Devices, Businesses Need an Apple Intelligence Policy](/blog/before-siri-ai-hits-work-devices-businesses-need-an-apple-intelligence-policy/).
* I also asked for honest feedback on this site in [Help Me Make This Blog Better](/blog/help-me-make-this-blog-better/). I enjoy writing here, but I want what I publish to be useful, readable, and worth your time.
* The latest [Security Signal Weekly](/blog/security-signal-weekly-june-13-19-2026/) covered FortiBleed, Splunk exploitation, Cisco Catalyst SD-WAN Manager, WordPress malware, AI search risks, and several other stories defenders should keep on their radar.
* On Mastodon, I shared a reminder that I created [EmojiCodec](/projects/emojicodec/), my VS Code extension for converting emoji formats, because apparently I am still excited about that one.
* I also boosted a note about nginx updates after new RCE fixes landed, because if you run nginx, it is probably a good time to make sure your update plan is not just vibes.

&#x270C;&#xFE0F; That is it for this week. I hope you have a great week and remember to be kind to each other!
