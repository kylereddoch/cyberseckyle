## Worth sharing

* I added two more parts to my Blue Team Fundamentals series. [First Response at Home](/blog/cyberseckyle-security-how-to-series-blue-team-fundamentals-part-5-first-response-at-home/) is about slowing down, preserving evidence, containing the problem, and recovering without making a suspected incident worse. [SIEM-Lite with a Few Useful Alerts](/blog/cyberseckyle-security-how-to-series-blue-team-fundamentals-part-6-siem-lite-with-a-few-useful-alerts/) shows how small environments can start with a handful of alerts they will actually review instead of collecting a wall of noise.
* The latest [Security Signal Weekly](/blog/security-signal-weekly-august-8-14-2026/) covers the stories defenders, IT teams, and MSPs should have on their radar, with the focus kept on active exploitation, patch urgency, and what to do next.
* I also shared a review of [Build Mode, a privacy-focused time tracker for macOS](https://www.pureapp.reviews/build-mode-time-tracker-for-macos/). It tracks focused work without watching your screen, which is a much better approach than the invasive employee-monitoring tools we keep seeing.
