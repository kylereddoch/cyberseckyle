> **Recap:** This past week wasn’t what I hoped for. I got sick right after Monday and ended up staying home the rest of the week. I didn’t get anything productive done. I mostly just slept.

## The vibe

Sick-all-week energy. Low battery. Blah. That’s the vibe.

## Highlights

* I’m not totally sure what triggered it, but I started getting this stomach pain, and I still have it. I went to the doctor on Thursday since it wasn’t improving. After what felt like 24 questions, a quick ultrasound, and a whole lot of pressing on my stomach, they still couldn’t give me a clear answer. The ultrasound did show *something* (possibly a hernia, but I’m not sure), so now I’m waiting on a referral for a more in-depth ultrasound.
* Because I was sick, I did very little this week. Mostly resting, and honestly struggling to get comfortable.
* I started a TV show called *The Lazarus Project*. I heard about it from Steve Gibson on the [Security Now](https://www.grc.com/securitynow.htm) podcast. It’s a time-travel thriller with some genuinely solid twists. Definitely worth watching. **It leaves Netflix on January 27th though, so don’t wait too long.**
* The best highlight of the week was the Seahawks winning the divisional round against the 49ers on Saturday. We went over to a friend’s house (who is a 49ers fan), and it was a great time watching the game. I can’t wait for the NFC Championship game this week against the Rams.
