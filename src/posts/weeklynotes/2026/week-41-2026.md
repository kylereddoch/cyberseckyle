## The vibe

Pretty worn out, honestly. I have wanted to keep things moving, but I have been tired enough that it is hard to ignore. Getting away from the desk for a while helped.

## Highlights

* &#x1F91D; I found [Buildside](https://www.buildside.app/go/245a2ea4d1) at the start of the month, and I have spent more time there as I get used to being a solo builder. I am still figuring out how to get my apps in front of people, so it helps to see how other builders talk about their work and what they are trying. I have met people working on their own projects and found things I would not have come across otherwise.
* &#x1F9ED; I have been reworking the marketing plan for [Trayage](https://trayage.app/). I updated the website based on feedback and have been trying different ways to show what the app does. Building it is one job. Helping someone understand why they might use it is another, and I am still working on that part.
* &#x1F34E; Trayage 1.1 is [in TestFlight beta](https://testflight.apple.com/join/BtqzAZem), and I kept working on it this week. The other big news came just after the week ended: version 1.0 finally [made it onto the Mac App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/trayage/id6810382134). After all the waiting and review work, it is good to have that option available alongside the direct download.
* &#x1F33F; All the app and launch work has taken a toll on my energy. I have been super tired lately, so on Saturday I got out of the house and touched some grass. LOL. It was good to do something that did not involve staring at another screen or thinking about the next thing on my list.
* &#x1F4FA; Some of my wife's shows started back up, and we watched a few of them together. YAY. It was a nice way to slow down for a bit.
