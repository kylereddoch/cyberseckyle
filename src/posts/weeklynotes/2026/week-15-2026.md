> I went full speed ahead this week.

## The vibe

Builder mode from start to finish. This week felt like creative momentum, shipping things I am genuinely proud of, and seeing several ideas finally become real. At the same time, getting sick in the middle of it all was a good reminder that even the most productive weeks still need room to slow down and recover.

## Highlights

* &#x1F4BB; I released a new "retro"-themed Eleventy starter template for anyone who wants to start or move thier blog to Eleventy. It is open source and available on [GitHub](https://github.com/kylereddoch/retro-garden-eleventy-theme). I am pretty proud of how it turned out, and I hope it helps some people get their blogs up and running faster.
* &#x1F418; I also released a huge update to my Catppuccin theme for Mastodon. You can read about the new update [here](/blog/catppuccin-complete-ui-for-mastodon-2026/) and grab the new code on [GitHub](https://github.com/kylereddoch/catpuccin-mocha-ui-mastodon).
* &#x1F517; I finally finished my first Eleventy plugin. It is called "Share on Mastodon" and allows you to add a Mastodon share button to your blog posts. It is open source and available on [GitHub](https://github.com/kylereddoch/eleventy-plugin-mastodon-share) and [npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@kylereddoch/eleventy-plugin-mastodon-share). I hope everyone finds it useful and easy to use.
* &#x1F389; A friend of mine in Mexico got married this weekend. I was honored to be invited (via Zoom of course). It was a beautiful ceremony, and I am so happy for them both.
* &#x1FAE9; I was out Wednesday and Thursday not feeling well. I had cough and congestion. I still have this dry cough that is hanging around.
* The weekend was pretty low-key. Just watched some shows and relaxed.
