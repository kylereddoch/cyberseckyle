> **Recap:** A mostly quiet back-to-routine week: school kicked off again, early tennis mornings returned, I binged some solid TV, and work was the usual security grind with one real troubleshooting headache.

## The vibe

Back-to-routine whiplash. The week was mostly steady and low-drama, but the early mornings hit hard, and everything had that “okay, we’re doing schedules again” energy.

## Highlights

- School started back up for me. This class is about Cloud Technologies, which I don’t work in every single day, but enough that it should be pretty smooth. Only 3 classes left.
- My youngest started back after winter break too, which means we’re back to 7am tennis practices. Waking up that early again was brutal.
- I finished *The Copenhagen Test* in two days. Great show. I’m already impatient for season 2.
- The wife and I started (and caught up on) *The Pitt*. This show is ridiculously good. Also, Noah Wyle in a medical drama again just feels right.
- The book is coming along nicely. I didn’t get as much time on it as I wanted, but progress is progress.
- Work was just another ~~usual week~~. Actually, last Thursday a client’s card terminals stopped reaching the internet, so payments were dead in the water. That was a pain to troubleshoot, but we tracked it down and got everything back online.
- The rest of the week was squashing alerts, killing malware, and generally keeping clients safe.
