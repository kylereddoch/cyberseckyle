## Worth sharing

* Passkeys are an improvement over passwords, but they are still vulnerable. I wrote about why passkeys are not a silver bullet for authentication security in [this post](/blog/passkeys-are-better-than-passwords-but-they-are-not-a-silver-bullet/).
* The FBI's Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3) released its 2025 Internet Crime Report, which provides a comprehensive overview of cybercrime trends and statistics. The report highlights the continued rise of ransomware attacks, business email compromise (BEC) scams, and identity theft. It also emphasizes the importance of public awareness and proactive measures to combat cyber threats. You can read the full report [here](https://www.ic3.gov/Media/PDF/AnnualReport/2025_IC3Report.pdf).
* ChatGPT's share link feature is being abused to deliver malware. Attackers are using the share link feature to create and distribute malicious content, which can lead to phishing attacks and other cyber threats. It is important for users to be cautious when clicking on shared links and to verify the source before engaging with the content. You can read more about this issue [here](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/chatgpts-share-link-feature-abused-to-deliver-malware/).
