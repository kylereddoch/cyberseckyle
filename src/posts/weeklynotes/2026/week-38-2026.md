## The vibe

A little worn out, but glad to be getting settled.

I am ready for the part where living in the new house means enjoying it without constantly thinking about what still needs to come out of a box. We are getting there, slowly but surely. Having some of the familiar comforts back helps, even if my stomach decided to make the week more difficult than it needed to be.

## Highlights

* &#x1F50E; Work was pretty interesting last week. I investigated an unauthorized ScreenConnect session on a client workstation after a malware alert flagged a fake `WindowsUpdate.exe`. The client did not even use ScreenConnect, so there was a lot more to work through than the quarantined file. I spent time connecting the endpoint evidence with firewall traffic and checking the rest of the environment. I wrote up the investigation, including the questions the available evidence could not answer, and linked it below.
* &#x1F6E0;&#xFE0F; I am also working on a new app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. The idea is to give people one place to keep the practical details of how their home works: which air filter to buy, where the water shutoff is, how to restart the internet equipment, and all the other things that tend to live in one person's head. I [shared the early idea on Mastodon](https://infosec.exchange/@cyberseckyle/117271385011257867) and asked for input. There is an early working prototype for text entries, with more of the details still being sorted out.
* &#x1F4E6; We are still getting things out of boxes and set up at the new house. Slowly but surely! I would very much like to be done unpacking, but at least more things are finding their place.
* &#x1F4F1; My wife's new iPhone 18 Pro came in, and I got it all set up. Hers is taken care of; I am still looking forward to preordering the [iPhone Duo](https://www.apple.com/iphone-duo/). October 16 cannot come soon enough!
* &#x1F4FA; We got the replacement TV for the one whose LED backlight went out. Dolby Vision and Dolby Audio are amazing! That has been a pretty nice upgrade while we are still getting the rest of the house sorted out.
* &#x1F39E;&#xFE0F; I finally got my Jellyfin media server all back up and working after the move. Between that and the replacement TV, I am happy to have that part of the setup working again. I have missed having my media collection ready to watch.
* &#x1F922; I also had a bout of a stomach bug, which was not wonderful. I could have done without that addition to the week. Blah!

It covers containment, the wider search for the same indicators, and why a clean second scan could not answer every question about what had happened on the workstation.

* [Security Signal Weekly: September 12–18, 2026](/blog/security-signal-weekly-september-12-18-2026/) went up on Sunday. This edition covers Cisco, ScreenConnect, GitLab, VMware, and other stories involving the systems businesses trust to manage access, infrastructure, and software.
