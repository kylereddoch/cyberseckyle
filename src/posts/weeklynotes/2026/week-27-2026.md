## The vibe

This was a quieter week overall. Not a whole lot of big updates, and I did not get as much time as I wanted to work on my iPhone or macOS app projects. There was still some World Cup watching, a little Linux tinkering, a family movie night, and time with friends, so it was not a bad week. Just one of those weeks that felt a bit scattered.

## Highlights

* &#x26BD; I have been following the World Cup and watching some of the matches. It was a real bummer to see Germany lose to Paraguay in a penalty shootout in the Round of 32. This team is definitely not the 2014 World Cup team. I am still a little sour about that one.
* &#x1F4F1; I did not get much time to work on the iPhone or macOS apps I have started. That was a little disappointing, but some weeks just do not leave as much room for side projects.
* &#x1F4BB; A lady came into work wanting us to look at her computer, but it was not updatable to Windows 11. She decided to get a new one and left the old one with us, a Lenovo ThinkPad T440s. I started turning it into a Linux machine and [checked out ParrotOS](https://infosec.exchange/@cyberseckyle/116852458383468042), but I ended up deciding to go back to Kali Purple.
* &#x1F3AC; Friday we went to watch *Toy Story 5* as a family. After that we went to a couple of stores, and I definitely did too much that day. It wore me out pretty good.
* &#x1F91D; Saturday I got to hang out with a couple of friends, which was really nice. After a heavier stretch lately, that kind of time was needed.

&#x270C;&#xFE0F; That is it for this week. I hope you have a great week and remember to be kind to each other!
