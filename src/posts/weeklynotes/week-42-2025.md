Last week was one heck of a week at work. Very stressful which bleed into my personal life which I never like. Add all this up and this was a week that I am glad is over. Here are my notes for the week.

### 💻 Work

Let's start off with work since that was the most stressful part of my week. I won't go into too much of the detail or rewrite it all, you can find the full store on my Mastodon post [here](https://infosec.exchange/@cyberseckyle/115381230745324239). But, long story short, a third-party vendor one of our clients use for their dealership that connects their printers to the vendors DMS system, rermoved all of the printers from both of the clients locations. They were unable to print anything from this system. This was a huge problem since this system is used to print all of the paperwork for the vehicles they sell. This included everything from the buyer's order, to the title work, to the registration forms, to the financing documents, and more.

In total, I spent about 3 days getting this set all back up and running. It was a total nightmare.

### 😎 Personal

As I mentioned earlier, work stress spilled over into my personal life this week. My mood was off most of the time, and I felt tense and generally unhappy. I tried to stay composed for my family, but it wasn’t easy. I hate when that happens because it’s not their fault, and it feels unfair to let my frustration affect them. I’m relieved the week is behind me and hopeful for a clean slate ahead.

Over the weekend, there was supposed to be a tennis tournament, my daughter’s first, but I found out she told her coach she wouldn’t be there because it conflicted with her choir regional tryout. She technically could have done both since it was an open tournament, but she chose not to. I was a bit disappointed, especially since I’d been looking forward to her first tournament.

### 🏎️ F1

This past weekend was the Circuit of the Americas in Austin, Texas, one of the tracks on my bucket list to visit. It marked Round 19 of the 2025 F1 season, and we’re getting close to the finish line. The race was exciting to watch, though not without some disappointment. With it being a Sprint weekend, Oscar (one of my favorite drivers, by the way) had two chances to pick up more points and extend his lead in the drivers’ championship, but things didn’t go as planned. The sprint race ended in disaster with a DNF and Max taking the win, and the Grand Prix wrapped up with Max winning again while Oscar finished in P5. Still, any weekend that includes F1 is a good weekend in my book.
