I have been looking for a way to get our family iCloud calendar to sync with an Android phone and Google Calendar. I finally found a solution that works and best of all, it is free. It is an app called [DAVx5](https://www.davx5.com). Weird name aside, it works great. It syncs our iCloud calendar and contacts with Google Calendar and Google Contacts. It also supports two-way sync which is great. If you are looking for a way to do this, I highly recommend it. Before anyone asks, it is for my youngest daughter, not for me. I am sticking with my iPhone and Apple ecosystem. 😆
