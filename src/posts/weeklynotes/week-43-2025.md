### 😎 Personal

I took a few days off for family health issues. On Wednesday, my mom was sent to the ER after an abnormal EKG at her appointment. My dad struggles with the medical details, so I drove over to help. By the time I arrived, they had tried medication to stabilize her heart rate and rhythm, but it was not working as hoped. They had to cardiovert her to restore a normal rhythm. She stayed overnight for observation and more tests.

Thursday, I had already planned to take my dad to his out-of-town appointment. After we returned, I visited my mom at the hospital. The team continued testing, including an echocardiogram and a stress test. They determined she had a prior heart attack at some point, though the timing is unclear. They want to do a scope, but are weighing timing because of her kidney issues. As of now, she is still in the hospital.

### 🎾 Last Tennis Tournament

Saturday was supposed to be the final tournament of the season. Check-in was 7:45 a.m. for an 8:30 start. It rained off and on the night before, and while the rain had stopped by morning, the courts were wet. They delayed the start and rolled the courts, but the cloudy, humid weather slowed the drying. Play began on half the courts after about an hour, and within 25 minutes it started sprinkling again. They called the tournament, and not even one full match was completed. I was disappointed, especially since my daughter missed the previous tournament and this one was canceled. There is always next season.
