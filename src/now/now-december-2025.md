It has been awhile since I did a "now" post. Ever since moving my website and getting that all sorted out, I have been meaning to get my /Now section put back together. Of course life, work, and school have kept me busy so it has taken a bit longer than I expected. But here we are!

## Work: tightening up the stack

Recently my boss and I have been working with our MSP tool vendor about getting some more of their tools integrated into our stack. I have been working on getting our systems better functioning together and our clients endpoints better protected. Automation is a big part of my current focus right now. Working on getting SaaS tools to work together better and automate tasks that we do on a regular basis.

## School: the final stretch energy

It has been a journey for sure. What started as a Bachelor's in Computer Science at SNHU has morphed into a Bachelor's in IT with a focus on Cybersecurity at University of Phoenix. I am now just 4 classes away from graduation which is wild to think about. The journey has been long and at times difficult, but I am so close to the finish line now. April can't come soon enough!

Online classes have been an interesting experience. I have learned a lot, but I am definitely ready to be done with school for a while after this. It is going to be an interesting thing to see what having my degree will do for my career in the coming year.
