I am a sucker for a good default wallpaper, and the new one for macOS 27 Golden Gate looks so good.

It is simple, colorful, and instantly feels like a Mac wallpaper. Honestly, it might be one of my favorite things to come out of WWDC26 so far. Sometimes all it takes is a new wallpaper to make your current computer feel new again. LOL.

Stephen Hackett has the [full-resolution Golden Gate wallpaper available to download](https://512pixels.net/2026/06/wwdc26-macos-27-golden-gate-wallpaper/). He also pointed out that, unlike the wallpapers from the last several versions of macOS, this one is a single image instead of having separate light and dark versions. I always use my Mac in dark mode, so I am curious to see how this one looks in both modes. I have a feeling it will look great in either.

Basic Apple Guy also has a couple of great WWDC26 options. He created [his own WWDC26 wallpaper](https://basicappleguy.com/haberdashery/wwdc26) from Apple’s early event artwork, and he has archived [Apple’s official WWDC26 wallpapers](https://basicappleguy.com/basicappleblog/wwdc26wallpapers) for the Mac, iPad, and iPhone. I have always liked his wallpapers, so I will probably end up rotating through those too.

I already know this one is going on my Mac.
