I’m ending The Defender’s Dispatch after 13 issues. Issue 013, published November 9, was the last one.

I started the newsletter to give practical cybersecurity and IT stories more room than a roundup allows. I’m glad I made it, but preparing the web edition and email every week became a source of stress. Some issues went out later than planned, and I don’t want to keep promising a schedule that’s wearing on me.

I wrote a [longer note about the decision](/newsletter/) on the Dispatch page. The [full archive](/newsletter/archive/) will stay online. I’ll keep writing here, including Security Signal Weekly. Thank you to everyone who read, subscribed, or sent a story.
