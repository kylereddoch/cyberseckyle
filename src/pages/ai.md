AI is part of my toolbox, but **it is not the author** of this website.

The ideas, opinions, and final words you read here are mine. I do not use AI to generate blog posts and publish them under my name. I do use it for some of the work around the site, much like I use other software that helps me get from an idea to a finished project.

## What I use AI for

### Coding and site features

I use AI coding tools to help me plan and build features for this site and other projects. That can mean tracing unfamiliar code, debugging a stubborn problem, writing repetitive boilerplate, or talking through a few possible ways to implement an idea.

The output is a starting point, not an automatic answer. I review the changes, run the builds and tests, and check the result in the browser. AI can produce code that looks completely reasonable and is still wrong.

### Editing help

I sometimes use AI the same way someone might use Grammarly: catching typos, pointing out an awkward sentence, or suggesting a clearer way to phrase something I have already written.

Those suggestions do not get the final say. I decide what sounds like me, what keeps the meaning intact, and what should be ignored.

### Image generation

I use image-generation tools for some featured images, illustrations, and visual ideas when a stock photo does not fit what I have in mind. When an image on the site was generated with AI, I identify that in the image caption.

### Technical problem-solving

AI is also useful as a second set of eyes when I am troubleshooting, learning an unfamiliar tool, comparing implementation options, or turning a rough technical idea into a plan I can test. It is especially helpful for getting past the blank-page stage of a project without pretending the first answer is the right one.

## The boundaries

I treat AI output as untrusted until I can verify it. It does not replace my judgment, make security decisions for me, or decide what gets published here. I also do not knowingly paste client data, credentials, private messages, or other sensitive information into public AI tools.

The short version is simple: **I use AI to help with the work, not to pretend the work happened without me.**
