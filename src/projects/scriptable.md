Scriptable is where I keep the smaller automations and utility ideas that are useful enough to earn a place on my phone.

I like projects like this because they are immediate. You build a script, put it on a device you use every day, and quickly learn whether it is actually helping or just adding noise.

## What lives in this repo

- Widgets and scripts that surface information I want at a glance
- Small experiments that make iOS feel more adaptable
- Utility work that is too niche for a full standalone app but still worth keeping around

## Why I keep it around

This repo is a good reminder that not every useful project has to be huge. Sometimes the best thing you can build is a small tool that makes one annoying part of daily life easier.
