Retro Garden is the project I built for people who want a site that feels personal from the beginning instead of something that looks like every other polished starter.

It leans into the parts of the web I enjoy most: a little personality, clear writing, and room for people to shape the site into their own thing. At the same time, I still wanted the build to feel modern and practical.

## What it includes

- A playful visual direction that still keeps readability and accessibility in focus
- IndieWeb-friendly publishing habits like webmentions, feed support, and person-first profile details
- Enough structure to launch quickly without boxing people into one exact look

## Why I keep working on it

Retro Garden is also a sandbox for ideas I want to try on my own site. When I improve the theme, I am usually solving a real publishing problem I have run into myself.

That makes it a useful project for both experimentation and reuse. It is meant to stay flexible, opinionated in the right places, and welcoming for people who want a more human-looking web presence.
