Brewventy started with a simple idea: make an Eleventy starter that feels warm, polished, and easy to pick up without demanding a lot of setup work before someone can publish.

It is the kind of project I wish more people had when they were ready to move from "I want a site" to "I actually launched one."

## What I wanted from it

- A themed starting point that already feels cohesive
- A structure that is approachable for people learning Eleventy
- Enough flexibility to grow into a real site instead of a throwaway demo

## Where it fits for me

Brewventy is more design-led than some of my other projects. It is not just about wiring up templates. It is about giving someone a launch point that already feels intentional the first time they open it.

That makes it a good counterpoint to Retro Garden. Both are starters, but they aim at slightly different moods and audiences.
